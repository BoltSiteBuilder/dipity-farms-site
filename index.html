Workshops

Plant, Grow & Gather

Join Sarrin in a garden workshop designed to bring a little more joy — and a little more garden — into your everyday life. These fun & educational workshops bring small groups together in the garden. The first season of workshops are being planned now — here's what's on the table.

  • Seed Starting for Spring

    Winter 2027

    Seed trays, lights, and timing - Oh My! In this workshop, guests will learn the basics regarding starting seeds. We will explore topics from seed starting trays, lighting, watering, timing, and more. After this class, you will be able to start your own vegetable transplants instead of buying them and take home your very first tray of veggie starts from seeds you plant in class.

  • Let's dig in!

    Spring 2027

    Roll up your sleeves with me and let's get our hands dirty. In this hands-on workshop, guests will learn about planting seeds and vegetables in the garden. From reading the labels and plant spacing to watering and much more, guests will develop knowledge and confidence that contribute to their future gardening success.

  • Earth Day

    Spring 2027

    Let's get together and play. This family oriented event will have a little something for big and little gardener's alike. Visit different stations around the garden and enjoy learning about composting, starting an earthworm farm, painting bug hotels, recycled crayon crafts and more.

  • Countertop Herb Garden

    SPRING 2027

    Bring the garden into your kitchen. In this workshop, guests will choose from a selection of culinary herbs and plant them in their very own countertop kitchen herb garden. Charming garden containers, a selection of culinary herbs, and organic potting soil are included. Guests will be invited to enjoy a few herb infused treats and will also take home a small selection of herb-inspired recipes to enjoy. $75 per person.

Meet the gardener

Hi, I'm Sarrin.

After walking through a season of life that changed my perspective in so many ways, I found myself drawn even more deeply to the simple, restorative rhythm of gardening. Dipity Farms became a way to turn a renewed sense of hope into something meaningful — a place to grow safe and nourishing food for myself and my family, to find peace and presence, and to build connection with my community.

Because even in the hardest seasons, there is always the possibility of growth, beauty, and hope.

Dipity Farms is rooted in the simple belief that, "to plant a garden is to believe in tomorrow!" What started in our backyard here in Greenville, SC — a small kitchen garden, a small flock of chickens, and a lot of love — has grown into a community centered garden business.

I humbly believe there is something powerful about planting a seed, watching it grow, and finding a little more joy in the everyday. I feel so blessed to be able to help other individuals, couples, and families build kitchen gardens to support and enrich their lives.

Dipity Farms has changed our lives and I have loved being able to share that love and light with others. That said, I look forward to having the opportunity to share the journey with you too!
